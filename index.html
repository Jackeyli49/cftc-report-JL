Methodology

Z-score = current value versus its 156-week mean and standard deviation, using open-interest-normalized positioning.

Weekly change = week-on-week contract change; the value in brackets is the z-score of that change.

Net position = long minus short. The automated package does not include prices; connect a separate price source to populate the Price column.

Crowding is flagged when any net, long or short z-score reaches 2.0; values at or above 2.75 are considered extreme.
